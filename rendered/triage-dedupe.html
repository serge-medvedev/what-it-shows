Triage: duplicate reports

owner support desk accepted reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

A busy morning brings hundreds of reports about the same thing. We keep one ticket per issue and merge every duplicate into it, so engineering sees one thread with a count.

The rule

A report is a duplicate only when evidence supports the same cause as an open ticket (decision 0002, replacing the subject-only rule):

  1. Read the body and look up the tenant’s locale and booking-form version.
  2. Check the input channel and compare causes using the shared logic in src/lib/kb/causes.ts.
  3. Merge supported duplicates; keep different causes apart. Investigate unknown tenants and unexplained symptoms before merging.
Subjects and browser-language intake tags are clues, not proof of the cause. Different causes can share a subject; the same cause can arrive under different subjects.

When you are unsure

Keep the report separate until you can establish the failing path. Preserve its body and tenant details so engineering can investigate. An unknown tenant does not prove a new cause.
